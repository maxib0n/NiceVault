Passphrase wordlists (7776 words each = log2(7776) = 12.925 bits per word)

en_eff_large.txt
  Source:  EFF "Large Wordlist" for passphrases (Joseph Bonneau, 2016)
           https://www.eff.org/files/2016/07/18/eff_large_wordlist.txt
           (dice numbers stripped; words unchanged, same order)
  Licence: Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 United States (CC BY 3.0 US)
           https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/us/
           Attribution: "EFF Large Wordlist" by the Electronic Frontier Foundation.

it.txt
  Source:  derived from "paroleitaliane" by Francesco Napoletano
           https://github.com/napolux/paroleitaliane
           files 60000_parole_italiane.txt, 1000_parole_italiane_comuni.txt, lista_badwords.txt
  Licence: MIT License, Copyright (c) 2016 Francesco Napoletano
           (the repository's only non-MIT file, coniugazione_verbi.txt, CC BY-SA 3.0, is NOT used)
  Built with make_it.py (same folder):
           python3 make_it.py 60000_parole_italiane.txt 1000_parole_italiane_comuni.txt it.txt lista_badwords.txt
           keeps lowercase a-z words of 4..8 letters ending in a vowel, drops the profanity list and
           most conjugated verb endings, prefers the common-words list, then length closest to 6,
           takes 7776 and sorts them.
